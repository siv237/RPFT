# No intrinsic selector of the central trace simplex
> Status: spec-frozen

Prove that the counting and equal-edge conventions give non-proportional
mass vectors. Prove that maximum entropy conditionally reproduces counting
density, type-preserving symmetry fixes a three-dimensional center,
unimodularity is independent of positive trace weights, gauge matching
requires an external target, Gibbs states use two independent gaps, and
vacuum stationarity is flat in all weight directions.
