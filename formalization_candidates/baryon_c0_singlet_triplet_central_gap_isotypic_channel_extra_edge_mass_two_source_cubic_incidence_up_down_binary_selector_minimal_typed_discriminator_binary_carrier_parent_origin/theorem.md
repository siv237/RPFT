> Status: spec-frozen

The endpoint projectors already generate a two-dimensional classical binary
algebra. The downward operator has gauge charge minus one and its GKSL
dissipator is covariant, but the invariant off-diagonal Hom is zero. The old
42-frame has zero extension on the new binary switch. A microscopic invariant
interaction requires an environment transition of charge plus one; the old
threefold charged-singlet carrier has no family-invariant vector. Therefore
the binary relaxation has no inherited microscopic parent.
