# Theorem

> Status: spec-frozen

A faithful carrier of two orthogonal incidence alternatives has dimension at
least two. On `C^2`, the controlled incidence projection has rank twelve and
is gauge-, Real- and grading-compatible. The typed Hamiltonian has gap seven
and, for positive coefficient, unique down-type ground state. Its unitary
dynamics preserves both populations, whereas the single jump `|d><u|` has
one-dimensional Heisenberg fixed algebra and unique down-type stationary
state.
