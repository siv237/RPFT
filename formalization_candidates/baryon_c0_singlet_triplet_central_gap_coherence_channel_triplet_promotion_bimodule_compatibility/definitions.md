# Definitions

- `P_Y=diag(0,0,1)`: central projector distinguishing `W_Y` from the
  isotypic pair `W_e,W_X`.
- `physical channel mixer`: an endomorphism commuting with `P_Y`.
- `triplet promotion`: an honest standard `SO(3)` action mixing all three
  channel directions.
- `minimal isotypic extension`: addition of `Z_R=(C,C,R)`, with old `Y_R`
  retained as a spectator.
