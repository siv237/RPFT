# Channel-triplet bimodule compatibility no-go
> Status: spec-frozen

Prove that the commutant of the current `2+1` endpoint-type projector is
`M2(C)+C`, and that its real orthogonal Lie algebra is only `so(2)`. Deduce
that neither the standard `SO(3)` nor standard `A4` triplet action is
admissible. Prove conditionally that one new isotypic endpoint produces a
canonical three-dimensional block supporting the required action, without
claiming inheritance of the old condensate.
