# Assumptions

- The candidate inventory is restricted to scalar/order-parameter carriers already registered in the project.
- The active `H15` endpoint content does not include the sterile-neutrino singlet extension.
- The rank-one coherence result `B*=sqrt(3)uv*` is used with unit vectors `u,v`.
- A symmetry-allowed portal is not treated as inherited unless its mixed parent derivative is nonzero.
