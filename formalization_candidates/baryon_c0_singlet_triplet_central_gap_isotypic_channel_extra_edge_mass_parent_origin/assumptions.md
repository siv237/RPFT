# Assumptions

- The three extra edges carry representations `(1,2)_{1/2}`,
  `(3,1)_{5/3}`, and `(3,1)_{2/3}`.
- Their Hermitian fluctuations act by the identity on each irreducible
  gauge multiplicity.
- Positive central weights are admissible until a simple factor or a unique
  trace-state is constructed.
- No cutoff moment or absolute spectral scale is imported.
