# Conditional trace parent and central-weight no-go
> Status: spec-frozen

Prove that the physical half-trace of the direct sum of the three Hermitian
edge blocks equals `2|z_Y|²+3|z_u|²+3|z_d|²`. Prove that positive central
weights change the coefficient vector to `(2p_Y,3p_u,3p_d)`, leaving two
projective relative freedoms. Prove that the gauge-index matrix has rank
three and therefore supplies no mass relation.
