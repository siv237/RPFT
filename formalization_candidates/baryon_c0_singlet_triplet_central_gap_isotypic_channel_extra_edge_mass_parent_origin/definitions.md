# Definitions

- `edge block`: the Hermitian matrix `[[0,z I_d],[conj(z) I_d,0]]`.
- `physical half-trace`: one half of the ordinary matrix trace on the
  Real-completed block.
- `central trace weights`: positive coefficients of the three pairwise
  orthogonal channel projectors.
- `gauge-index matrix`: the three-by-three matrix of abelian, weak, and
  colour indices of the extra edges.
