# Definitions

- `E_coh = Lambda²W*`, with `W=C2+C1` under the physical channel group.
- `E_fam = R3`, the standard family representation.
- `current intertwiner`: equivariant under the full channel/family product group.
- `promoted intertwiner`: equivariant after replacing `W` by an oriented diagonal `SO(3)` triplet.
- `*`: the metric-and-orientation Hodge map `Lambda²R3 -> R3`.
