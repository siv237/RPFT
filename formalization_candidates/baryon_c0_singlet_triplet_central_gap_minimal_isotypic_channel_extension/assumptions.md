# Assumptions

- Isotypy with `e_R,X_R` includes their canonical gauge representation and
  hypercharge `-1`.
- Physical anomaly cancellation is computed on independent Weyl fields.
- Strict first order allows an edge exactly when opposite grades share at
  least one bimodule coordinate.
- The current left charged-singlet multiplicity has no declared nontrivial
  channel-`SO(3)` action.
