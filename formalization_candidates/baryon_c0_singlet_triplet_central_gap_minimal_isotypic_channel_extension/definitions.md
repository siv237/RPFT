# Definitions

- `Z_R=(C,C,R)`: proposed third right endpoint isotypic to `e_R,X_R`.
- `physical vectorlike completion`: independent Weyl pair `Z_L+Z_R`, not
  the formal particle-antiparticle Real double.
- `selected edge set`: `L_L-Z_R`, `Y_L-Z_R`, and `Z_L-Z_R`.
- `mass selector`: a rank-two map `C3_R -> C2_L` together with its
  one-dimensional right kernel.
