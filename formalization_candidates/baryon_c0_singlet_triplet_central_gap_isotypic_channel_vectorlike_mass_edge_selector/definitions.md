# Definitions

- `B`: rank-one coherence field in `Hom(C3,C2)`.
- `M`: rank-two vectorlike mass field in `Hom(C3,C2)`.
- `P_B=B*B/3`: right coherence projector.
- `P_M=M*M`: right mass-row projector after imposing `MM*=I2`.
- `aligned subparent`: the four-term nonnegative functional coupling `B`
  and `M` through `||MB*||²`.
