# Aligned coherence-mass projector theorem
> Status: spec-frozen

Prove that the zero set of the four-term nonnegative functional has a
rank-one coherence projector and a complementary rank-two mass projector,
so the mass kernel equals the coherence line. Prove transverse positivity
on the real slice and prove that restoration of six omitted complex edge
fields leaves twelve additional zero modes, preventing a full-graph
selector claim.
