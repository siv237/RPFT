# Minimal pair of central Hamiltonian gaps
> Status: spec-frozen

Prove that quotienting three central energies by a common shift leaves two
gaps. Prove that their beta-products map diffeomorphically to the interior
of the positive two-simplex with Jacobian `p_Y p_u p_d`. Prove strict Gibbs
convexity and prove that equilibrium leaves the absolute energy, mass, and
relaxation scales unidentified.
