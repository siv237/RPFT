# Algebraic portal shape from the cubic moment
> Status: spec-frozen

Prove that tracelessness uniquely gives
`Qhat=diag(-3/4,0_6,(1/4)I3)`, that
`Tr(Qhat D_B²)=-5T/8`, and that
`Tr(D_B+lambda Qhat)³=-15lambda T/8-3lambda³/8`. Also prove that the
inherited even moments contain no term linear in `lambda`.
