# Definitions

- `D_B`: the odd Hermitian operator of the `1-6-3` coherence chain.
- `T = Tr(BB*)` and `d = det(BB*)`.
- `Qhat(q) = diag(-3/4,q I6,(1/4)I3)`.
- `X = D_B + lambda Qhat`.
- `typed corner intertwiner`: an equivariant identification of the coherence three-corner with the active family triplet.
