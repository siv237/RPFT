$$\mathcal C_G=\alpha_1Y^2+\alpha_2C_2(SU(2))+\alpha_3C_2(SU(3)),\quad\alpha_i>0.$$

$$\ker\mathcal C_G=(\mathcal E_{\rm new})^{G_{SM}},\qquad P_G=\mathbf1_{\{0\}}(\mathcal C_G)=\lim_{t\to\infty}e^{-t\mathcal C_G}.$$
