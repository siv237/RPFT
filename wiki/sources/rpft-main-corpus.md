## Summary

`RPFT-main/` is the broad markdown corpus for the RPFT research program. It contains rigorous derivations, English variants, deductive-logic chapters, exploratory notes, older drafts, and AI prompts.

- `RPFT-main/deductive_logic/` — staged deductive interpretation and assembly.
- `RPFT-main/Проработка/` — exploratory development notes.
- `RPFT-main/base/`, `RPFT-main/ai-promts/`, `RPFT-main/habr/`, `RPFT-main/peper/` — supporting prompts, posts, and paper drafts.
