# TOE

> Status: working
> Type: concept
> Updated: 2026-08-11

## Summary

TOE is a correlation-operator architecture whose primary object is a compact
self-adjoint integral operator `Chat` with Gaussian heat kernel on `M x M`.
The metric on the emergent four-manifold `M` is reconstructed from the
logarithmic Hessian of the kernel. TOE may describe the same deeper structure
studied through [[ugsm]], but its carrier topology is not fixed by the primary
document.

## Key Points

- Provides the non-UGSM side of the [[toe-ugsm-bridge]].
- Uses `M x M` as the two-point kernel domain, not as a physical product
  compactification.
- Separates the emergent spacetime `M`, finite NCG space `F`, and conjectural
  generation carrier `M_int`.
- Does not derive `K=RP3 x S1`; see
  [[zero-prompt-toe-carrier-trace-2026-08-11]].
- The first post-`K` carrier classification selects round `S4` conditionally
  from a declared compact homogeneous positive-Einstein spin menu; see
  [[version4-toe-native-s4-carrier-candidate-gate]].
- The normalized Gaussian-state audit gives `S4` larger Rényi-2 correlation
  entropy than equal-radius `S2 x S2` across the tested profile; see
  [[version4-s4-s2xs2-correlation-purity-gate]].
- The normalized-state Gibbs completion derives the entropy sign from
  quantum relative entropy and gives `S4` lower free energy; see
  [[version4-gibbs-free-energy-carrier-gate]].
- The literal action `Tr f(Chat/Lambda^2)` has a trace-class obstruction for
  standard cutoffs. The corrected EFT dictionary uses
  `H_C=-log(Chat)/tau`; see [[version4-spectral-gibbs-equivalence-gate]].
- Releasing the `S4` volume gives an opposite-boundary no-go: Gibbs free
  energy decompactifies while a positive cutoff spectral action collapses;
  see [[version4-s4-radius-boundary-no-go]].
- Free energy per four-dimensional correlation cell conditionally stabilizes
  `S4` at `a/sigma=1.3513921957` and realizes `p=-epsilon`; see
  [[version4-correlation-cell-free-energy-density-gate]].
- Primary gravitational matching gives a formal sub-Planck radius but fails
  its own large-cutoff condition on the selected carrier; see
  [[version4-absolute-scale-eft-validity-gate]].
- A metric `RP3` internal factor nearly restores old pi-scales but produces
  product collapse and KK towers; only its discrete `Z2` core survives
  cleanly in the current hybrid; see [[version4-s4-rp3-hybrid-experiment-gate]].
- The archive uses distinct kernel, normalized-state and spectral-density
  entropies. Their signs cannot be merged into one topology principle. The
  remaining unexecuted route is the TOE 6.5 joint spectral-density/carrier
  variation; see [[version4-project-retrospective-entropy-measure-gate]].
- The scalar carrier comparison has not yet been promoted to a full quantum
  vacuum comparison. The required scalar-vector-ghost-Dirac fluctuation
  determinant is isolated in [[version4-negative-space-bundle-fluctuation-gate]].
- A cross-topology full-field determinant is itself underdetermined until
  finite Einstein, Weyl-squared and Euler/topology weights are fixed; see
  [[version4-full-field-carrier-counterterm-gate]].
- Treating the Gaussian spectral action as a fundamental bare Wilsonian
  boundary fixes those weights. Its exact Dirac trace conditionally agrees
  with the S4 correlation scale; see
  [[version4-gaussian-bare-spectral-topology-gate]].
- Current workspace framing suggests TOE and UGSM may be two shadows of one [[spectral-correlational-source]].
