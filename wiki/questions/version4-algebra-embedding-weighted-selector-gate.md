## Coarse Equivalence

When the observed/hidden algebra acts trivially on family multiplicity, an
explicit node-wise orthogonal transformation maps the two remaining branches.
It commutes with the coarse algebra, grading and doubled real structure. Its
determinant is minus one, explaining the reduced Pfaffian sign flip.

## Enriched Embedding

Promoting the derived family translations and shear to represented algebraic
data blocks that equivalence. The branches then differ through
algebra-decorated spectral moments even though their unweighted spectra agree.

## Conditional Selector

The positive weighted moment Tr(P_minus D^4) equals 83/4 and 79/4 and selects
the plus branch. This is a genuine eigenfunction-sensitive distinction, but
the principle selecting the weighted state is not yet derived.
