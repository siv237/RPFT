> Status: working
> Type: question

The four transposition minima are exactly the four edges of the sector graph
K2,2 between leptons and quarks. A complete 16-element orientation-cocycle
menu defines degree-normalized vertex incidence operators. Mass-only training
selects one branch and produces a Cabibbo-scale first angle, but misses the
middle-generation masses, the other CKM angles and the Jarlskog invariant.
