# Version IV.SM One-Ratio Family Functional Gate
> Status: working
> Type: question
> Updated: 2026-08-15

A single shared ratio t in the Casimir-decorated functional R4-tR2 is trained
only on four quark mass ratios. The optimum lies near 4 but misses the middle
generations by factors up to 22.6. The subsequently opened CKM angles and
Jarlskog invariant also fail. A broad scan finds no simultaneous factor-ten
candidate, closing the common quadratic/quartic ratio as a flavour repair.
