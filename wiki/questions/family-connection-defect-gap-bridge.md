The antisymmetric family operator is generated by a real `SO(3)` covariant
derivative, not by a scalar Majorana Yukawa term:

```text
D = D_vortex tensor I3 + Gamma_s tensor (d_s + K_n/L),

```

Projection onto the unique transverse vortex zero mode gives the required

## Exact Spectrum

For a product tube and constant longitudinal connection,

```text
lambda^2 = epsilon_j^2 + ((2 pi k + sigma theta_*)/L)^2,
sigma in {-1,0,+1}.
```

Since `0 < theta_* < pi`, the only zero is `(j,k,sigma)=(0,0,0)`. Hence the
full separable BdG operator has exactly one real zero mode and gap

```text
delta_sep = min(Delta_perp, theta_*/L).
```

For a local connection on a length-`pi` tube, `theta_*/pi =
0.7979250094...`. This local result does not imply a flat ambient extension.

## Stability

Class-D perturbations with operator norm below `delta_sep/2` preserve a
rank-one low-energy spectral projection; particle-hole symmetry pins its
single eigenvalue to zero. A blockwise Feshbach sufficient condition is

```text
theta_*/L > b^2/(Delta_perp-q).
```

## Flat Torsion Obstruction

The systolic loop represents the order-two element of
`pi1(RP3 x S1)=Z2 x Z`. A flat ambient connection must therefore obey
`W_gamma^2=I`, allowing only rotation angles zero or pi. The required
`theta_*=2.506755...` is neither. Hence the same flat Wilson connection
cannot live on the systolic core.

## Two Consistent Routes

1. Put the flat family Wilson connection on the non-torsion external `S1`
   and regard the full defect support as `gamma x S1`. For a periodic total
   spin branch the exact-one theorem survives with family gap
   `theta_*/(2 pi)=0.3989625047...`. The anti-periodic branch has no axial
   zero and is therefore an explicit spin-structure gate.
2. Keep the connection on `gamma`, but allow curvature or a singular source.
   For an abelianized curvature along `n`, the required condition is
   `integral F_n = 2 theta_* mod 2 pi`; its principal value is
   `-1.2696746116...`. Both flux and axis alignment must be derived.

## Compensated Spin Branch

For an anti-periodic geometric spin structure, an odd-charge Majorana mode
has momentum

```text
p = [2 pi (k+1/2) + q alpha + sigma theta_*]/L1.
```

A charge-two condensate leaves the residual flat choices `alpha=0,pi`.
For odd `q`, `alpha=pi` cancels the spin sign while remaining invisible to
the charge-two condensate. Thus the required spin-G branch exists without a
new continuous parameter. Its dynamical selection over `alpha=0` remains
open.

The minimal local charge-two Higgs action cannot make this selection. The
flat configurations `(a,Phi)=(0,v)` and
`(a,Phi)=(pi ds/L1, v exp(2 pi i s/L1))` both have `F=0`, `D Phi=0` and the
same potential energy, while unit-charge fermions prevent them from being
gauge equivalent. A nonlocal Wilson term, topological coupling or odd-charge
quantum determinant is required.

## Hosotani Sign

For

```text
I_rho(beta)=log[(cosh(2 pi rho)-cos(2 pi beta))/(cosh(2 pi rho)-1)],
```

`I_rho` increases from `beta=0` to `1/2`. Bosons contribute `+I/2` and
fermions `-I/2`. Anti-periodic odd-charge fermions and periodic bosons both
give `V(pi)-V(0)>0`, so the minimal one-loop spectrum selects `alpha=0`.
For a periodic boson with `q=1/3`, the correct final shift is `1/6`, not
`1/3`; the sign conclusion is unchanged.

The large-rho expansion is

```text
I_rho(beta) ~ 2[1-cos(2 pi beta)] exp(-2 pi rho).
```

Thus `I(1/2)~4e^-`, `I(1/3)~3e^-`, and `I(1/6)~e^-`.

## Geometric vs Total Periodicity

The compensated `N^c` has geometric anti-periodic shift `eta=1/2`. It only
becomes total-periodic at `alpha=pi`. Its determinant therefore compares
`beta:1/2 -> 0` and contributes `+I(1/2)/2`, favoring `alpha=0`. It is not
the periodic-spin fermion that would give the negative sign.

For one minimal B-L generation with a common rho, twelve fractional-charge
and four odd-charge anti-periodic Weyl components give

```text
Delta V_gen = 8 I(1/2) - 6 I(1/3) > 0.
```

Even artificially retwisting one `N^c` to geometric periodicity leaves
`7 I(1/2)-6 I(1/3)>0` at equal mass.

For the actual minimal field content, unequal masses do not reopen the gate:
every anti-periodic odd-charge term is `+I_rho(1/2)/2`, every fractional
fermion term is `+[I_rho(1/2)-I_rho(1/3)]/2`, and every periodic fractional
boson term is positive. Their sum therefore selects `alpha=0` for arbitrary
positive `rho_i`. A genuinely new geometric-periodic odd-charge sector is
required.

## CS/Theta No-Go

An ordinary quantized Euclidean CS or theta term contributes a topological
phase. It vanishes on a fully flat configuration; with fixed flux it gives a
phase such as `i k m alpha`, not a negative contribution to the real Wilson
potential. It therefore cannot turn the Hosotani maximum at `alpha=pi` into
an energetic minimum.

Real stabilization needs additional periodic odd-charge fermions with enough
multiplicity, another real Wilson potential, or an explicit TQFT projection
that removes the `alpha=0` sector.
