## Order-One Result

For a finite bimodule edge H_ij to H_kl, the double commutator is
proportional to (a_i-a_k) D (b_j-b_l). A nonzero edge therefore requires
i=k or j=l.

In the minimal observed/hidden four-node menu, the two diagonals are
forbidden and the four rectangle edges are allowed. A triangle is impossible;
the minimum order-one connector cycle is a square.

## Real-Structure Phase

A self-adjoint J-compatible square has

- Tr(D^2)=4a^2+4b^2;
- Tr(D^4)=8a^4+8b^4+16a^2b^2 cos^2(phi);
- det(D)=4a^2b^2 sin^2(phi).

For a positive quartic coefficient the phase minima are phi=+-pi/2.
Reality does not erase the loop phase, but it leaves the two orientations
degenerate.

## Remaining Obstruction

Order one factorizes gauge intertwiners from family multiplicity matrices.
It does not restrict those family matrices. Their effective products span
the full M3(C) unless a separate parent selector restricts them to the
rank-one/incidence algebra.
