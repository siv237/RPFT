## Question

Do the spectral functions already admitted by the program uniquely force the Majorana condensate and select one winding orientation?

## Results

- In the one-amplitude reduction, lambda v squared equals minus f prime at zero.
- The linear function gives no stable condensate, the unit exponential is critical, and the remaining tested exponential and rational functions condense.
- The collective norm 23 plus inverse pi fixes a positive configuration metric but not the sign of the quadratic potential coefficient.
- The two lowest momenta plus one and minus one have identical even spectral invariants.
- A real action built from the squared Dirac operator cannot split the two orientations.
- The previously tested eta phase does not generate a real splitting in the minimal vectorlike model.

## Verdict

The present parent action predicts neither the existence of the Majorana condensate nor its orientation. A fundamental spectral function, its scale, and a derived real orientation-odd term are still required.
