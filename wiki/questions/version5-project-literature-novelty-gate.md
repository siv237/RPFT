# Version V project literature and novelty gate

> Status: working
> Type: question
> Updated: 2026-08-15

## Problem

Prevent Tome V from reopening a mechanism already closed inside the project
or presenting a standard literature ingredient as project novelty.

## Project-wide verdict

The ingredients of all three Version V classes have strong precedents:

- Gibbs/relative entropy, information geometry, heat traces, spectral
  actions and compact-saddle determinants;
- `SO(3) -> A4`, Wilson-line flavour breaking, Hosotani dynamics,
  order-three vortices, fixed-charge rotors and Majorana defect modes;
- spectral Pati--Salam, generalized fluctuations, junk quotients,
  auxiliary fields and torsion-induced four-fermion channels.

The novelty target must therefore be a derived conjunction, not an
ingredient.

## Surviving N2-level questions

1. Can one correlation operator define a joint state/carrier functional,
   its fluctuation determinant and finite topology weights before carrier
   comparison?
2. Can one boundary trace derive fixed charge, condensate, family axis,
   exact-one Majorana kernel and an SM-sensitive second sector?
3. Can a bounded finite-geometry classification derive the required
   relative sign and full-rank Hessian before phenomenology?

## Closed loops

- fixed-`K` uniqueness and entropy-only topology selection;
- topology comparison with free finite counterterms;
- another rank-one Pati--Salam potential or product enhancement;
- ordinary `Tr D^4`, singular junk recovery or Pfaffian repair of the
  family moment map;
- Hosotani reopening with the already rejected minimal field content;
- HS or torsion invoked without a derived kernel and new action;
- threshold or Yukawa scans before parent closure.

## Protocol

Every new gate must include a dated literature delta, its closest internal
predecessors, a known/specialization/new-theorem table and an explicit stop
rule. Absolute priority claims remain forbidden without specialist and
bibliographic-database review.

## Next gate

Rewrite Gate V.0 dossiers so that each names its standard ingredients and
one exact N2-level conjunction. Start with the carrier dossier; use the
boundary dossier as an independent short falsification control.
