> Status: working

## Summary

Analyzes the residual symmetry of the two generation axes left after
minimizing the factored family functional.

## Result

The two surviving axes correspond to the directions `(e1 - e2)` and
`(e0 - e3)` in the four-state carrier. The centralizer of both factored
operators has order `4` and coincides with the Klein translation group.
The element `(01)(23)` maps one axis to the other and conjugates the
transposition `(12)` into `(03)`. The two minima therefore form one exact
projective orbit; their energy equality is not accidental.
