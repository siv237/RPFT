## Result

The relative chain admits an exact 20-complex-dimensional KO6 completion and
the physical half-trace preserves `4 det(Delta Delta^dagger)`. However, an
ordinary direct-sum finite spectral triple would interpret those 20
components as additional fermionic states. The non-propagating branch must
therefore remain a relative bosonic/KK cycle rather than a physical Hilbert
summand.

At the stabilized rank-one Delta background the general Yukawa quadratic
form is
`H_Y=-4||Y||^2+8||M_R^dagger Y||^2 <= 0`.
For the project Yukawa seed and the traceless B-L adjoint direction, all
eight real phi modes are negative. The 15 Hermitian traceless Sigma modes are
flat and all Delta/phi/Sigma mixed Hessian blocks vanish.

The combined Delta, fixed-point auxiliary, phi and Sigma signature is
`(43 positive, 24 zero, 8 negative)`.

## Verdict

The determinant selector repairs Delta rank growth but not the full
composite vacuum. The next admissible route is a connected
representation-sensitive phi/Sigma interaction or a genuine two-scale
spectral action.
