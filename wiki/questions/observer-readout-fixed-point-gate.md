# Observer Readout Fixed-Point Gate

> Status: working
> Type: question
> Updated: 2026-08-06

## Summary

The observer/readout hypothesis is mathematically viable but not yet
predictive. Nonreducing unital completely positive compressions can create
effective noncommutativity from commuting source operators, while the unique
fully affine-covariant readout is too coarse to produce family hierarchy or
mixing.

## Exact Results

- Finite observations never identify a unique readout: a polynomial
  perturbation can vanish on every known point and change an unseen point.
- Every unital readout preserves central scalars, \`R(cI)=cI\`.
- The \`S4\` twirl has fixed algebra \`span{P_singlet,P_triplet}\`.
- Its triplet restriction is scalar, so it cannot select masses or CKM.
- Its invariant density matrices form a continuum.
- The canonical affine-triplet compression preserves factor commutativity.
- All 256 deterministic generic rank-three UCP compressions produced a
  nonzero commutator and effective mixing.

## Interpretation

The missing layer may be the observable subalgebra and its conditional
expectation. But a freely chosen compression is another continuous selector.
The state and observable algebra must be derived before physical data load.

## Next Gate

Derive \`omega\`, identify a modular-invariant observable subalgebra \`B\`,
construct the unique \`omega\`-preserving conditional expectation, and only
then calculate blind observables.
