## Pair Scan

For eight real symmetric incidence operators, all 28 unordered up/down pairs
were tested. Their commutator norms are distributed as

- 10 pairs with norm 0;
- 6 pairs with norm 1;
- 8 pairs with norm 4;
- 4 pairs with norm 6.

A positive cross coupling favors commuting pairs. A negative one favors four
degenerate maximally noncommuting pairs. Neither sign yields a unique pair.

## CP No-Go

Every operator is real symmetric, so every commutator is real
antisymmetric and `Tr([Hu,Hd]^3)=0`. The real incidence menu cannot generate
CP violation.

The minimal next route is the orientation-odd Hermitian operator
`(P-P^dagger)/(2i)` of a directed three-cycle.
