## Remaining Gaps

and the algebra does not fix M. A generic quartic-minus-quadratic spectral
polynomial fixes the radius through a coefficient ratio.

The real even action also leaves two exactly degenerate CP-conjugate
branches. An independently derived orientation-odd contribution is required
to choose a sign.
