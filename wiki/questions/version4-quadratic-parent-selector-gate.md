# Version IV.SM Quadratic Parent Selector Gate
> Status: working
> Type: question
> Updated: 2026-08-15

## Result

The 12 successful affine directions reduce to eight distinct Hermitian
incidence operators. Their commutator norms against the two translations,
the shear and the rank-one projector form four score classes of multiplicity
two. No quadratic score class is a singleton.

A metric-weighted test with weights `1`, `1/4`, `1/5` has two degenerate
minima. These minima commute, so assigning them to different Yukawa sectors
still produces a common eigenbasis and no CKM mixing.

## Verdict

The simplest quadratic parent selector is closed negatively. A cross-sector
or orientation-sensitive invariant is required.
