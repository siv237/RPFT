# Version V foundational relative-architecture gate

> Status: working
> Type: question
> Updated: 2026-08-15

## Clue

Tome I says that the fundamental entity may be a “category of reductions”
and that the primary object is the law of admissible transitions rather than
an isolated thing. Later work mostly formalized the Hilbert/operator pieces
but replaced the correlation-assembly rule by attempts at one absolute
action.

## Project pattern

The strongest surviving structures are relative or compositional:
determinant ratios, holonomies, mapping cones, relative quotient norms,
determinant lines, defect indices and residual cycles. Repeated failures
concern absolute topology weights, scales, one-trace actions and common
normalizations.

## Candidate architecture

Use a category/groupoid of admissible reductions:

- objects: geometric, spectral, correlation or boundary realizations;
- morphisms: correspondences, relative spectral triples or superconnection
  transports;
- composition: a gluing/Kasparov-type product;
- weight: a relative cocycle or determinant-line-valued functor.

Under a local normalization change `B(X)`, an edge changes by
`Gamma(X->Y) -> Gamma(X->Y)+B(Y)-B(X)`. Open paths retain endpoint
ambiguity, while closed-loop sums are invariant. The deterministic audit
checks both additive and multiplicative versions exactly.

## Reduction triangle

The minimal project diagram is

`R_g -> R_s -> R_c -> R_g`.

- geometry-to-spectrum: partial;
- spectrum-to-correlation: exact through the heat/log dictionary;
- correlation-to-geometry: missing;
- triangle cocycle: consequently undefined.

This identifies the missing carrier prior as a missing reconstruction
morphism rather than merely one absent coefficient.

## Verdict

The relative/category hypothesis is admitted as a bounded research direction
but is not a mathematical architecture pass. It explains the project pattern
and yields a precise next obstruction; it does not yet produce physics.

## Resolution by the next gate

[[version5-reduction-triangle-cocycle-gate]] instantiated the three objects
and found an exact inverse-spectral obstruction. A weak correlation object
does not determine geometry; a correlation object enriched by a full
spectral triple makes the return morphism tautological. The minimal triangle
therefore fails and `Omega_gsc` remains undefined.

Boundary-source now returns as the next control and possible supplier of
non-tautological locality/gluing data.
