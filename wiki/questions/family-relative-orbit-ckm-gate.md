# Family Relative Orbit CKM Gate

> Status: working
> Type: question
> Updated: 2026-08-15

## Summary

Tests whether the residual relative orientation of the upper and lower
sectors can produce the physical CKM mixing without free parameters.

## Result

After factorizing by the residual group, two relative classes remain:
matched and orthogonal representatives of the axial orbit. For the minimal
operator `M_s = L(w3,w1) + g A_{ns}`:

- the matched class gives unit mixing;
- the orthogonal class gives only a real rotation of the first two
  characters, `tan(theta_mix) = g(1 - c*)/(2(w3 - w1))`, while the third
  generation does not mix;
- the coefficient `g` is not derived, so a full parameter-free CKM
  derivation is not achieved.
