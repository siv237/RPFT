## Preregistered Menu

With f0=f(0)=1 and f2=integral f(u)du:

- sharp cutoff: f2/f0=1;
- heat profile exp(-u): 1;
- Gaussian exp(-u^2): sqrt(pi)/2;
- second heat profile (1+u)exp(-u): 2.

At Lambda R=1 all profiles pass the nonzero-vacuum inequality, but they
give three different values of chi R.

## Continuous Degeneracy

The normalized family exp(-a u) has f2/f0=1/a. Rescaling a and Lambda
leaves (f2/f0)(Lambda R)^2 invariant. Spectral width and cutoff
normalization are one continuous orbit, not separately observable data.

## Verdict

Natural positivity, smoothness and decay conditions do not select a unique
spectral moment. One train scale must fix the invariant
zeta_mom=(chi R)^2+1/2.
