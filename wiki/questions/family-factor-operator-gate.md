## Result

The two carrier factors induce commuting translations on the four-state menu.
Restricted to the nontrivial character triplet, they jointly distinguish all
three family directions. Translations plus the geometric shear generate an
order-eight action whose triplet commutant has dimension two, so full symmetry
still leaves a `1+2` decomposition.

The strongest tested parameter-free hierarchy is the additive winding pattern
`(exp(-2*pi), exp(-pi), 1)`. Its log RMS is `0.591` for declared down-quark
masses, `1.342` for charged leptons, and `3.754` for up quarks. It is a useful
candidate kernel, not a universal mass theorem.

## Mixing No-Go

Any up/down Yukawa operators that are functions only of the two commuting
translations share one eigenbasis and predict `CKM=I`. A viable action must
derive a sector-dependent noncommuting shear insertion before comparison with
mixing data.
