# Version V architecture selection gate

> Status: working
> Type: question
> Updated: 2026-08-15

## Problem

Select one primary Version V research class without treating selection as a
mathematical or physical pass and without using observed numbers.

## Protocol

The gate uses a lexicographic rather than weighted score:

1. do not import a failed Version IV action;
2. state one N2-level conjunction;
3. define a binary pre-phenomenological gate;
4. bound the initial comparison/model class;
5. prefer the route addressing the project-wide measure/vacuum gap with the
   smallest new representation ontology and an explicit candidate
   functional.

## Verdict

- **Primary:** carrier-first / corrected TOE 6.5.
- **Independent control:** boundary-source / Wilson--defect.
- **Deferred:** new finite geometry until a finite complexity bound exists.

The carrier route is selected only for the next gate. It has not fixed its
functional semantics, finite gravitational/topology weights, full Hessian or
two-sector normalization. Therefore mathematical passes and physical
closures both remain zero.

## Freeze consequences

- Boundary or finite-geometry coefficients may not be imported into the
  carrier functional.
- The carrier comparison class must be frozen before ordering calculations.
- Gibbs and positive bare spectral actions may not be combined with a new
  weight.
- Masses, couplings, `S_vac` and mixing data may not select the sign or
  counterterms.

## Next gate

`version5_carrier_measure_freeze_gate` must freeze the comparison class,
choose one primary functional semantics, derive or forbid finite curvature
and topology weights, specify statistics/vector completion and define the
joint Hessian operator.

## Follow-up

[[version5-carrier-measure-freeze-gate]] closes the current carrier-first
realization negatively: fixed-carrier Gibbs state normalization passes, but
the source does not derive a carrier prior, unique Gibbs/bare semantics,
finite topology weights or the full field/BV measure. Boundary-source is
therefore activated as the next short control gate.

[[version5-boundary-parent-trace-freeze-gate]] also closes negatively:
the Wilson coefficient pair passes only at operator level, while one parent
trace, charge/source selection and the joint condensate–axis–Majorana
derivation fail. The remaining admitted class is new finite geometry after a
strict complexity bound.

[[version5-finite-geometry-complexity-bound-gate]] supplies the bound.
[[version5-real-selector-leaf-ko6-gate]] then corrects its first graph-level
candidate and admits an active-family rectangle for one direct test.
