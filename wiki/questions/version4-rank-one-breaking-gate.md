# Version IV.SM Rank-One Breaking Gate
> Status: working
> Type: question
> Updated: 2026-08-15

## Result

- The mod-two cohomology ring has only the identity and one nontrivial shear
  automorphism.
- On the affine family triplet the shear has spectrum `(-1,+1,+1)`.
- Its odd projector `(I-S)/2` has rank one and spectrum `(1,0,0)`.
- A leading mass operator proportional to this projector gives one heavy and
  two massless families.

## Mixing No-Go

The two factor translations commute and are simultaneously diagonal on the
triplet. Up/down operators built only from them share an eigenbasis and cannot
predict nontrivial CKM mixing.

The next admissible ingredient is a derived sector-dependent noncommuting
incidence operator.
