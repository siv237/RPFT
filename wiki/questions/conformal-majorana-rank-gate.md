## Question

Can removal of one gravitational conformal or gauge mode explain 24-1=23 in the Majorana module?

## Linear Gauge Test

For a metric Fourier mode,

    delta h_mn = k_m xi_n + k_n xi_m.

At nonzero momentum the map has rank four, but a pure trace tensor is not in its image. At zero momentum the symbol vanishes, so the constant volume mode is not generated by a periodic diffeomorphism. Equivalently, the integral of div xi vanishes on a compact manifold without boundary.

## Direct-Sum Test

The frozen field space is Sym2(T*K) direct_sum R24_Majorana. Diffeomorphism BRST acts as xi -> (G xi,0). Dividing by gravitational gauge volume therefore leaves the Majorana rank equal to 24.

Wrong-sign conformal contour rotation is not a quotient. A genuine Weyl quotient would require an exact Weyl symmetry of the massive action and still would not automatically select one real direction in R24.

## Verdict

The 1/24 coefficient in the A-hat genus is not a component count. Criterion F.3 fails for the conformal/gauge-volume proposal. The only explicit rank-one route remains the conditional class-D defect on the systolic geodesic, whose complement in R24 has rank 23.

## Reopening Condition

Derive either a complete Weyl/BRST complex with a rank-one mixed image in the Majorana fiber, or derive the existing odd-winding Majorana defect from the parent action.
