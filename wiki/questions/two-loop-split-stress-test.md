## Sign Audit

Running from `Lambda` down to a lower scale obeys

```text
alpha_i^-1(low)=alpha_i^-1(high)+(b_i/2pi) log(Lambda/low).
```

Thus positive matter contributions increase the low-energy inverse couplings. The earlier exploratory shortcut subtracted `(Delta b/2pi) S_split`, giving the opposite sign. It also changed the train anchor from `alpha_em^-1=137.0360` to `132.2454`.
