## Explicit Coefficients

After spin trace, the Phi-dependent terms are:

    a2: -4 Tr Phi^2
    a4: 2 Tr(D Phi)^2 + 2 Tr Phi^4 + (R/3) Tr Phi^2.

a0 has no Phi dependence.

After scalar kinetic normalization, the potential is fixed by

    chi^2 = (f2/f0) Lambda^2 - R/12.

For RP3_R x S1_R:

    (chi R)^2 = (f2/f0)(Lambda R)^2 - 1/2.
