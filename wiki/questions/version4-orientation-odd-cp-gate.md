## Result

For every directed three-cycle define

`X_P(r) = (P+P^dagger)/2 + r (P-P^dagger)/(2i)`.

Its triplet spectrum is

`1, -1/2 + sqrt(3) r/2, -1/2 - sqrt(3) r/2`,

so the orientation-odd component removes the twofold degeneracy for generic
nonzero `r`.

## Physical CP Test

All 28 pairs were checked symbolically with independent real `r_u,r_d`.
For the physical squared-mass matrices,

`Tr([X_u X_u^dagger, X_d X_d^dagger]^3) = 0`

identically in every pair.

Complex matrix entries are therefore rephasing-removable in this linear
menu. A third noncommuting layer or a genuine cross-sector orientation
invariant is required.

At unit orientation weight the 28 pairs produce only a family permutation or
large democratic `1/3`--`2/3` overlap patterns, not hierarchical CKM-like
mixing.
