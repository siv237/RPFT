# Version III Dual Architecture Verdict

> Status: working
> Research status: both paths tested; neither is a closed UV completion
> Type: question
> Updated: 2026-08-10

## Base-K Path

The full compact determinant is mathematically valid, but four-dimensional
renormalization permits finite local terms lambda2 R chi^2 and lambda4
chi^4. Their finite parts can move a stationary point to any chosen
positive chi. The determinant therefore does not predict the absolute
vacuum until the parent action supplies a subtraction condition.

## Internal-K Path

All four product spin structures and all unitary flat characters are
covered by the same Lichnerowicz estimate. Since R_K=6,

    D_K^2 >= R_K/4 = 3/2.

The geometric twisted Dirac operator has no kernel in every flat branch.
The frozen internal field menu therefore cannot produce the two finite
four-dimensional Dirac pairs.

## Verdict

The base-K branch survives as a four-dimensional effective theory with an
open quantum renormalization condition. The internal-K flat spinorial
branch is closed negatively and can reopen only with a new nonflat
curvature or defect mechanism.

## Priority

Test whether one spectral renormalization condition fixes the finite
counterterms of the base-K determinant. Keep a nonflat internal lift as a
separate future model.
