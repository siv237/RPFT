## Result

For the algebra C plus C, the first-order condition allows a Dirac edge only
when the source and target bimodules share a left or right index. Connecting
the two diagonal factor sectors therefore requires an intermediate
off-diagonal sector. Reality closure adds its transpose and produces the
minimal four-sector Krajewski square.

The square admits a nonzero odd real-compatible Dirac operator. Its chiral
block contains two complex parameters. Full rank requires a nontrivial
relative complex phase.

## KO Gate

The natural checkerboard grading commutes with index-transposition reality.
It therefore does not have the KO-dimension-6 sign. Keeping the diagonal
factor sectors in KO dimension 6 requires particle--antiparticle doubling to
eight finite states and a derived physical half-trace.
