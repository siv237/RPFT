# Weighted Gap Yukawa Readout Gate

> Status: working
> Type: question
> Updated: 2026-08-06

## Summary

Factor-spectrum gaps provide a source-derived unequal A3 edge metric. With
an exponential readout this is the first tested noncommuting construction
to place four light quark ratios within factor five, but it fails blind CKM.

## Results

- Tested 12 weighted graphs and 108 mass readouts.
- The unique factor-five candidate uses inverse-square gaps 3/4 and 1/4.
- The composed chord has weight 3/16.
- Both sectors select beta = 2pi.
- Mass errors are 3.675, 4.426, 1.090 and 1.315.
- Blind J is too large by factor 540.36.
- CKM angle ratios are 3.76, 6.59 and 129.92.

## Next Gate

Derive two correlated sector-relative noncommuting graphs from one finite
action instead of assigning one sector to a chain and the other to a chord.
