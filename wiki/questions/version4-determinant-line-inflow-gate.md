## Pfaffian Line

Each connector-circle branch has an explicit nonzero Pfaffian section. Both
sections have winding minus three, but the existence of the sections already
trivializes the complex line bundle.

## Branch Interpolation

The two real-sign endpoints are connected by an explicit complex path whose
determinant modulus is at least one. There is no zero crossing and no spectral
flow, so the reduced sign is not a global Z2 anomaly.

## Inflow Verdict

The messenger sector is vectorlike and anomaly-free, and the finite family
graph supplies no spacetime bulk operator or APS extension. No nontrivial
Dai-Freed inflow selector exists without adding a new chiral or bulk sector.
