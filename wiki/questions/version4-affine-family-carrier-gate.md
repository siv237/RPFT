# Version IV.SM Affine Family Carrier Gate
> Status: working
> Type: question
> Updated: 2026-08-15

## Result

The four spin structures form an affine torsor over `F2^2`. Promoting the
full `AGL(2,2)` action to a family symmetry gives the full permutation group
`S4`.

- the group order is 24;
- its commutant on `C4` is `span{I,J}`;
- `P1=J/4` is the uniform singlet projector;
- `P3=I-J/4` is a canonical rank-three projector;
- the sum-zero triplet is irreducible under the full affine action.

Tensoring this triplet with the 16-state generation atom gives three
identical anomaly-free generations.

## Limitation

The full affine symmetry is a new family postulate, not a diffeomorphism
symmetry of the frozen geometry. Exact symmetry also leaves the three
generations degenerate; hierarchy and mixing remain open.
