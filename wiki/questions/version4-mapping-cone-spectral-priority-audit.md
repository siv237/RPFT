# Version IV mapping-cone spectral priority audit

> Status: working
> Type: question
> Updated: 2026-08-15

## Question

Which parts of the project mapping-cone spectral Hessian are standard
literature mechanisms, and which part may be a project-specific
specialization?

## Literature result

Mapping-cone determinant lines, Schur-complement Gaussian reduction,
log-determinant Hessians and inverse-gap fidelity susceptibility all have
strong independent precedents. None of those ingredients is a project
novelty by itself.

The targeted search did not find the exact conjunction of:

- periodic spectrum `(pi n)^2` with the zero mode removed;
- primitive incidence `b_theta=(1-U_theta)u`;
- a real-Gaussian Schur block;
- Hessian coefficient `-(1-cos theta)/45`;
- interpretation as a Wilson two-point response rather than the full
  finite-source determinant.

This absence is not proof of publication priority.

## Safe claim

The admissible wording is that the project found a possibly previously
unremarked specialization of known mapping-cone and log-det-Hessian
mechanisms. Absolute novelty claims require independent MathSciNet, zbMATH,
INSPIRE, Crossref and specialist review.
