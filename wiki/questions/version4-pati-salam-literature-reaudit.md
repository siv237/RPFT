## Correction

The earlier wording merged two distinct scalar scenarios:

- General fundamental branch: `Sigma=(2_R,2_L,1+15_4)` and no independent
  weak-singlet adjoint. Its canonical potential fails the vacuum test.
- Composite first-order branch: fundamental fields include
  `phi=(2_R,2_L,1)`, `Delta=(2_R,1_L,4)` and conditionally
  `Sigma_4=(1_R,1_L,15)`. The adjoint exists, but the larger fields are
  composite products and the full breaking potential was not established.

## Singlet correction

The algebraic no-go applies only to a decoupled singlet or one universal
quadratic mass shift. A connected real singlet on a diagonal Dirac block may
generate additional mixed invariants and remains an open literature route.

## Project correction

The project has constructed the observed Standard Model finite algebra, but
not yet an explicit Pati–Salam finite Hilbert space, grading, real structure
and Dirac matrix. Claims about “the project Pati–Salam grading” are therefore
targets, not completed objects.

## Next gate

Build the explicit project Pati–Salam finite block, reproduce both the
fundamental and composite branches in one convention, derive the composite
adjoint rather than impose it, and test a connected diagonal singlet before
returning to RG.
