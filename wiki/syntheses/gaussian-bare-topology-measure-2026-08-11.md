# Gaussian bare topology measure

> Status: conditional positive reopening
> Updated: 2026-08-11

## Finding

The finite-counterterm obstruction can be bypassed at the bare matching
scale if the Gaussian spectral action is taken as fundamental rather than as
an asymptotic mnemonic for a renormalized determinant. Its moments and the
Weyl-squared-to-Euler ratio are then fixed.

The exact spin-Dirac action has one carrier crossing at
t=0.124153169935769. The correlation-cell scale maps to t=0.10673404,
inside the S4 window. Thus scalar correlation and spinorial bare-action
tests agree conditionally.

## Limits

This does not yet close the physical vacuum. The cutoff/correlation relation
must be derived, and quantum RG flow has no supertrace cancellation in the
current field content.
