## Main finding

The project is blocked because several distinct variational principles were
compressed into one word, “entropy.” Almost all old carrier and normalization
mechanisms are already covered by existing no-go gates.

The exception is the TOE 6.5 program that varies spectral density itself,
adds a fluctuation determinant, and requires a second-variation test. It was
proposed but never executed as one normalized parent problem.

## What remains exact

- The defect-cycle Hodge Gram operator `diag(pi, pi^-1)`.
- The discrete `Z2` topology and linking data of `RP3`.
- The conditional `S4` ordering in normalized Gibbs-state comparison.
- The correlation-cell stationary ratio `a/sigma=1.3513921957`.

None alone supplies the missing common measure.

## Recommended program

Return to the original correlation-source idea, not to the original fixed
carrier. Freeze the comparison class, use `H_C=-log(Chat)/tau`, vary the
normalized state and carrier jointly, derive `Gamma_fluc` from the same
Hessian, and demand a positive joint second variation before comparing any
observable number.
