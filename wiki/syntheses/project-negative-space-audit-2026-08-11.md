> Status: working

## Result

The earlier retrospective found surviving positive structures. This audit
instead maps the assumptions under every major dead end.

One genuinely forgotten geometry was recovered and tested: the nontrivial
circle bundle over `RP3`. It produces exact parity-momentum locking but its
scalar free-energy density still collapses.

The most important untested route is not another topology. It is the full
renormalized field fluctuation determinant on the competing carriers. The
current `S4` preference is a scalar correlation-state result, not yet a
gauge-ghost-fermion vacuum theorem.

## Priority gaps

1. Full field-content `Gamma_fluc` on `S4` versus `S2 x S2`.
2. Joint state/carrier Hessian using that same determinant.
3. Warped or bundle geometries only if the determinant derives a cross term.
4. Finite admissible single-vertex algebra as a separate local problem.

The follow-up [[version4-full-field-carrier-counterterm-gate]] sharpens item
one: before evaluating spectra, the parent must fix finite Einstein,
Weyl-squared and Euler/topology coefficients. Otherwise any determinant
ordering can be reversed by an allowed finite local counterterm.
