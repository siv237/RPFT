# Project Overview

> Status: working
> Type: synthesis
> Updated: 2026-07-09

## Summary

The project studies a cluster of theories and documents around [[rpft]], [[ugsm]], [[toe]], and a possible [[spectral-correlational-source]] that may generate them as different projections or languages. The existing catalog frames RPFT as the broad meta-framework, UGSM as a stricter calculable spectral sector, TOE as a correlational/architectural reading, and the audit files as stress-tests of their compatibility.

## Current Working Hypothesis

The most useful current hypothesis is that RPFT, UGSM, and TOE are not competing fragments but partial descriptions of one deeper structure. This candidate structure is presently called the [[spectral-correlational-source]] until a more precise mathematical formulation is fixed.

## Research Layers

- [[rpft]] — broad ontological layer: geometry, topology, compact sectors, and admissible structures.
- [[ugsm]] — calculational spectral-geometric layer: operators, coefficients, observables, and reductions.
- [[toe]] — correlation/architecture layer: alternative language for the same or overlapping structure.
- [[toe-ugsm-bridge]] — integration layer: comparison and translation between TOE and UGSM.
- [[holonomy-and-dirac-sectors]] — verification layer: stress-tests across Dirac, spin, gauge, and enriched sectors.
- [[s2t-reinterpretation]] — naming/reframing layer for the still-emerging common entity.
