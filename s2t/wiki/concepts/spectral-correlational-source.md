# Spectral Correlational Source

> Status: draft
> Type: concept
> Updated: 2026-07-09

## Summary

The spectral-correlational source is the provisional name for the deeper object that may underlie [[rpft]], [[ugsm]], and [[toe]]. It is not yet a finalized mathematical entity; it is a research placeholder for a common operator/geometric/correlational source suggested by bridge and audit documents.

## Key Points

- It should explain why spectral, geometric, topological, holonomy, and correlation descriptions appear to align.
- It should clarify whether TOE and UGSM are equivalent projections, complementary approximations, or only partially overlapping models.
- It must eventually constrain operators, sectors, admissible states, scales, and observables.
- It should preserve contradictions and failed checks rather than absorbing them rhetorically.
