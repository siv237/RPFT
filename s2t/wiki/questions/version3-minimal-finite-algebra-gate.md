## Positive Result

minimal faithful representation supplies two components with equal trace
weights. Embedding each component with the normalized zero mode of the
spectator factor gives the exact RP3 and S1 norms without free weights or
chosen defect points.

This closes the factor-measure gap and the doubled-module-origin gap inside a
minimal spectral reduction.

## Red Team

For the simple diagonal representation, complex-conjugation real structure
and an off-diagonal finite Dirac operator, the first-order double commutator
is proportional to

    mixing * (a_C-a_X) * (b_C-b_X).

It vanishes for arbitrary algebra elements only when the mixing is zero.
Thus the minimal model supplies kinematics but no vertex joining the two
factor components.
