# Version IV.SM Gauge-Casimir Family Split Gate

Endpoint weights built from the normalized gauge Casimirs distinguish left
and right representations. Exact spectral equality leaves a down/electron
anchor degeneracy. Using the frozen low-scale gauge train produces four
distinct anchors without flavour coefficients, but the resulting quark
mixing is large and the mass hierarchy fails. The structural split passes;
the blind flavour test does not.
