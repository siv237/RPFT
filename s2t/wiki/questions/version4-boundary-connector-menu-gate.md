# Version IV.SM Boundary Connector Menu Gate

## Requirements

A single-source boundary package must provide an off-diagonal finite edge, a
nonzero portal, family-triplet breaking, a rephasing-invariant oriented loop
and anomaly safety.

## Restricted Result

- direct sum: no bridge;
- one scalar triplet: portal and family breaking, but no spectral edge or CP
  loop;
- one vectorlike pair: bridge, but no family carrier or physical phase;
- one triplet messenger chain: no closed rephasing-invariant phase;
- two messenger paths plus one family-triplet connector: first package that
  can satisfy all five capabilities.

## Status

This is a graph-level capability result, not yet a real spectral triple. The
charge ledger, order-one condition, loop-phase survival and coefficient
normalization remain open.
