# Version IV.SM Vectorlike Messenger Chain Gate

## One-Sector Construction

Two vectorlike messenger pairs with hidden charges `0` and `1`, a family
triplet scalar of charge `-1`, and a hidden scalar of charge `+1` admit the
three required gauge-invariant vertices. Vectorlike left/right contributions
cancel all local anomalies.

Integrating out the messengers produces the dimension-six operator

`bar(F_L) H Omega phi_h f_R /(M0 Mq)`.

## Phase Count

A single chain is a tree and has zero rephasing-invariant phases. Two
nonparallel paths have cycle rank one and leave one physical loop phase.

## Sector Obstruction

The four Dirac Yukawa sectors require four different Standard Model singlet
representations. A single messenger representation cannot cover them all.
Either four correlated packages or a new unified messenger multiplet is
required.
