## Summary

The current S2T corpus does not yet recover the minimum established content
of our world as a closed independent multisector theory. This does not mean
that the missing sectors have no numerical representation: the collective
pi atlas already compresses eleven rows across gauge, mass, mixing and
cosmological sectors.

- Two-loop split running gives residuals of about `+2.06%` for `M_W`,
  `+0.96%` for `M_Z`, `-7.62%` for the weak-angle proxy and `-32.05%` for

- The blind symmetric CKM texture gives `J=0.051041`, versus the frozen PDG

- The tau relation is extremely close but still depends on an unproved seed
  and projection normalization.
- The neutrino splitting ratio is close, but the integer `23`, absolute mass
  scale and PMNS matrix are not derived.
- The Higgs mass bridge inherits the conditional absolute scale and does not
  yet predict the measured coupling and width pattern.

## Missing Established Sectors

- Complete charged-lepton and quark mass spectra.
- CKM and PMNS matrices from a selected finite Dirac/Yukawa operator.
- Simultaneous electroweak and QCD precision closure.
- Confinement, hadron spectrum, decay widths and scattering amplitudes.
- One quantum-consistent parent action with a common measure.
- Dynamical gravity and cosmological background evolution.
- Quantitative missing-mass, accelerated-expansion and baryogenesis sectors.

## Verdict

The gate fails: there are no closed independent multisector physical
predictions. This does not refute the primary-image hypothesis; it shows that
the current reduction is not yet sufficient to identify our world uniquely.
