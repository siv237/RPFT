# Version IV.SM Orientation Spectral No-Go

## Exact Isospectrality

The two surviving noncommuting branches have identical characteristic
polynomials for both D and D squared. All ordinary moments through the matrix
dimension agree, and Cayley-Hamilton extends the equality to every higher
moment.

Therefore no real spectral function Tr f(D squared) can select the
orientation.

## Other Bosonic Tests

The tested graded even traces vanish in both branches. Their determinant
magnitudes are equal, and the CP-odd loop observable W sin(theta) is zero at
both minima.

## Surviving Routes

Chern-Simons/anomaly-inflow term, or departure from the real-even spectral
