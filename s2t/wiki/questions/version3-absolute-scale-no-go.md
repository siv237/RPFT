## Result

## Radion Test

A single homogeneous radion term has no isolated positive minimum. A
two-term potential such as A R + B/R fixes R through sqrt(B/A), so the
coefficient ratio must itself be derived. Setting R=1 is only a choice of
dimensionless units unless a duality or dynamical equation enforces it.

## Consequence

Until a dilaton/radion or dimensional-transmutation mechanism is built, the
program may predict only dimensionless ratios, phases, ranks and coupling
relations. One absolute mass may be used as a scale-setting input but cannot
also count as a blind prediction.
