# Formalize Common Source

> Status: draft
> Type: question
> Updated: 2026-07-09

## Question

How can the provisional [[spectral-correlational-source]] be turned into a mathematically constrained object rather than a narrative bridge between RPFT, UGSM, and TOE?

## Why It Matters

Without formalization, the bridge can remain suggestive but underdetermined. A useful formulation should specify operators, admissible states, sectors, mappings to observables, and criteria for falsifying the proposed unity.

## Candidate Subquestions

- What is the minimal operator or category of operators that generates both TOE-style correlations and UGSM-style spectral data?
- Which audit results are essential evidence, and which are artifacts of the test setup?
- Are TOE and UGSM equivalent projections, complementary approximations, or only partially overlapping descriptions?
- What would count as a decisive contradiction?
