# Pati-Salam Composite Potential Hessian Gate

> Status: mature
> Type: question
> Updated: 2026-08-13

## Summary

The minimal composite branch fails its vacuum gate. The Standard-Model
rank-one Delta condensate has nine Goldstone zero modes but six negative
Hessian modes. The potential instead prefers a rank-two condensate with the
wrong unbroken subgroup.

## Key Points

- Preserving `SU(2)_L` requires `phi=0`; the composite formula then makes the
  adjoint `Sigma_4` disappear from the finite operator and remain flat.
- Physical half-traces reduce exactly to
  `half Tr D_F^2 = rho^2` and `half Tr D_F^4 = tau^2`.
- The normalized potential is `V=-rho^2+tau^2`.
- Rank one: Hessian signature `(positive, zero, negative)=(1,9,6)`.
- Rank two: Hessian signature `(4,12,0)` and lower energy.
- Any singlet correction depending only on `rho` leaves the rank-growth mode
  negative.
- A viable extension must derive a rank-sensitive determinant invariant or an
  independent connected-adjoint coupling.
