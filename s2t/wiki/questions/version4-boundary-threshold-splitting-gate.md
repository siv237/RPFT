# Version IV.SM Boundary Threshold Splitting Gate

## Linear Result

For a complete vectorlike SO(10)-type spinor package, the ansatz
`log(M_a/M0)=eta q_a` gives zero one-loop differential threshold direction
for `q=Y`, `B-L` and `T3R`. The cancellation is the mixed
gauge-gauge-generator anomaly identity of the complete spinor.

Thus one linear boundary order parameter cannot repair the missing triple
gauge crossing.

## Quadratic Diagnostic

The first nonsilent menu `log(M_a/M0)=eta q_a^2` can be inverted to force a
one-loop intersection, but this is not a prediction. For the two messenger
pairs required by the portal chain, the smallest component spreads are
approximately:

- `Y^2`: `2.51e9`;
- `(B-L)^2`: `5.88e19`;
- `T3R^2`: `1.25e3`.

## Verdict

The linear single-cocycle route is closed. The quadratic `T3R` branch is the
least extreme surviving diagnostic, but it requires a parent action that
fixes both the nonlinear mass law and its coefficient before comparison with
gauge data.
