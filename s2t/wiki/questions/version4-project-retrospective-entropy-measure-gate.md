# Version IV: project retrospective entropy-measure gate

> Status: fixed-carrier routes exhausted; spectral-density variation reopened
> Updated: 2026-08-11

## Problem

The complete RPFT, UGSM, TOE and tractate corpus was reread to determine
whether a mechanism stronger than the exhausted fixed-`K` parent action had
already been proposed and then forgotten.

## Corpus result

- The inventory snapshot contains 1188 files including generated artifacts and 848
  principal `tex/md/py/pdf` files.
- Only six exact duplicate groups were found.
- Early RPFT preloads `RP3 x S1` before a full variational comparison class.
- Later strict branches expose the quotient, spin, measure and normalization
  assumptions.
- TOE 6.5 contains the genuinely unfinished route: vary spectral density,
  fluctuation determinant and information entropy in one functional.

## Entropy correction

The archive uses three inequivalent notions: maximum coordinate/kernel
entropy at fixed variance, entropy of a normalized heat state, and minimum
spectral entropy or complexity when comparing carriers. These have different
domains and constraints.

## Numerical obstruction

For unit-volume `S4` and `S2 x S2`, mean spectral energies cross at
`tau=0.0957664481672465`. Minimum mean energy is therefore not a scale-free
topology selector.

## Factorized-measure no-go

The natural early-RPFT completion
`-(log Z4 + log Z3)/(N4 + N3)` is a cell-number-weighted average of separate
densities. It cannot have an interior hybrid minimum and removes `S4` at the
global boundary.

## Reopened route

Use `H_C=-log(Chat)/tau` and vary
`F[rho,M]=Tr(rho H_C[M])+Gamma_fluc[rho,M]-S(rho)/tau` over normalized states
and a preregistered carrier class. The fluctuation term must come from the
same Hessian, not a fitted sector coefficient.
