# Version IV.SM Family Square Spectral Selector Gate

## Restricted Operator

The order-one square is restricted to one edge pair carrying the rank-one
projector and the other carrying one of the eight Hermitian incidence
operators. The common finite potential is minimized over the loop phase and
the two edge amplitudes.

## Exact Selection

Every candidate has phase minima at plus or minus pi/2. After amplitude
minimization:

- four transposition operators give vacuum coefficient 2;
- four Hermitian three-cycle operators give vacuum coefficient 3/2.

The spectral square therefore selects the transposition conjugacy orbit.

## Remaining Obstruction

All four transposition directions have identical spectral moments and form
one residual orbit. The gate reduces the menu from eight operators to four,
but it does not select a unique family direction or an up/down assignment.
