# Version IV.SM Pfaffian and Eta Orientation Gate

## Reduced Pfaffian

In plus-minus chiral ordering, A_red=gamma D is antisymmetric. The two
isospectral branches have det(M)=+3/2 and -3/2, hence reduced Pfaffians
-3/2 and +3/2. An oriented reduced fermion measure therefore sees a relative
phase pi.

## KO6 Cancellation

The full J-paired form is A_red plus minus its conjugate. Its Pfaffian is
the squared magnitude 9/4 in both branches. Integrating both formal copies
removes the orientation phase.

## Remaining Measure Gap

The reduced Pfaffian sign flips under an odd Grassmann basis permutation.
No determinant-line orientation is currently derived. The determinant can
also rotate continuously without crossing zero when the connector phase is
complex, so the sign is not a topological invariant of the full complex
configuration space. Both eta invariants vanish.
