## Result

For the connected one-copy chain, the commutant of the fixed-point node
algebra together with the chain operator is only the scalars. For `k`
identical coefficient-free copies, the commutant is `M_k(C)` and has complex
dimension `k^2`. Irreducibility therefore selects `k=1` and fixes
`lambda_rel=1`.

A `k>1` model can become irreducible only by adding noncommuting copy-space
operators. That is a new finite model rather than an unexplained identical
multiplicity and requires its own axiomatic and physical audit.

The uneliminated `Delta+C` Hessian has 52 real variables and signature
`(43 positive, 9 zero, 0 negative)`. Eliminating the 36-dimensional
fixed-point auxiliary block by its Schur complement returns the exact
effective spectrum `8 sqrt(2) (1), 2 sqrt(2) (6), 0 (9)`.

## Verdict

The coefficient-free classical mapping-cone branch fixes its one-copy
weight and has no hidden auxiliary instability. The next gate is the KO6
embedding and the remaining `phi/Sigma_4` mixed Hessian.
