# Version IV.SM Higgs-Yukawa Gate

## Structural Result

The minimal observed finite algebra yields one complex Higgs doublet. An
exhaustive scan of 16 left-scalar-right combinations leaves exactly four
renormalizable Dirac Yukawa vertices:

- `Qbar tilde(H) u`;
- `Qbar H d`;
- `Lbar H e`;
- `Lbar tilde(H) nu`.

The one-doublet scalar potential contains only `H^dagger H` and its square.
A sterile right neutrino also admits a Majorana mass; without it, neutrino
mass first appears through the dimension-five Weinberg operator.

## Predictive Limit

For three generations the four arbitrary complex Yukawa matrices contain 72
real entries before flavour-basis quotient; a complex symmetric Majorana
matrix adds 12. The finite algebra fixes the graph, not these values.

## Boundary Condition

The scalar hidden portal is gauge allowed but its coefficient is not fixed;
the minimal direct sum gives zero. A future boundary action must derive the
portal, family breaking and CP orientation from one connector representation,
not independent fitted coefficients.
