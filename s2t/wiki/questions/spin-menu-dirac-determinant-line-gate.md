# Spin Menu Dirac Determinant Line Gate

## Cellular Dirac

The canonical weighted square incidence complex gives
`D=[[0,B],[B*,0]]`. Its vertex Laplacian has spectrum
`(0,1/pi,2/pi,3/pi)` and commutes exactly with both factor translations. It is
the previously audited commuting family Laplacian, not a mixing selector.

## Determinant Values and Transport

Eta invariants and determinant values attached separately to the four spin
structures define diagonal weights unless parallel transport between their
determinant lines is added.

On the `Z2 x Z2` menu, connection topology reduces to two projective classes:
trivial plaquette holonomy or `pi` flux. The first is commuting. The second has
two doubly-degenerate cellular-Laplacian levels and mixes the uniform state with
the triplet.

## Verdict

Cellular Dirac, eta weights and determinant-line holonomy reduce to cases
already closed by the factor and projective audits. None selects one of the
twelve outside-D8 full-M3 incidence directions.

Reopening requires a new non-flat boundary connection that fixes its graph,
edge magnitudes and up/down sector assignment before flavor data are used.
