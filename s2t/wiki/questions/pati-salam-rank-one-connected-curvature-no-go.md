# Pati-Salam Rank-One Connected Curvature No-Go

> Status: current finite geometry closed
> Type: question
> Updated: 2026-08-14

## Sigma Obstruction

The selected vacuum supplies one rank-one color projector P. Every
quadratic connected word containing P vanishes on the embedded su(3)
adjoint Q Sigma Q. Consequently connected contractions see only seven of
the fifteen Sigma directions and retain an eight-dimensional kernel.

For a rank-r projector the unavoidable kernel has dimension
(4-r)^2-1. Full rank first becomes possible at r=3, while the current
2x4 Delta carrier has rank at most two and its selector chooses rank one.

## Phi Obstruction

The complete tested span of connected Majorana-Yukawa maps has real rank
six on the eight-dimensional phi tangent, leaving a two-dimensional common
kernel.

## Verdict

The current one-copy finite geometry is closed as a physical vacuum
mechanism. Reopening requires a new rank-at-least-three diagonal carrier or
several independently derived noncommuting color projectors.
