# Tome I to Tome II Traceability

Status: audit added on 2026-07-10.

Tome I contributes an ontological and methodological kernel, not a finished numerical model. The reread found that Tome II already covered the spectral/geometric parts, but did not explicitly document several Tome I theses.

## Covered Explicitly

- Admissibility over locality: topology, spin structures, holonomies, boundary conditions, and determinant rules.
- Operator generation: spectra, zeta residues, heat-kernel terms, and EFT functionals.
- Geometric realizability: the carrier `RP^3 x S^1` and its normalization tests.
- Anti-overfit discipline: permitted inputs, no-go criteria, ablation, and permutation controls.

## Newly Documented in Tome II

- Primacy of structure: observables are treated as stable spectral/EFT regimes.
- Multilayer reality: geometric, spectral, topological-phase, and EFT layers are mapped into Tome II chapters.
- Single-source thesis: technically represented by the shared carrier, spectra, and EFT reductions, but not proven as a full reconstruction of `S`.
- Invariant kernel: represented by `(K, Spec, D_train, L_EFT, Q_audit)`.
- Model selection: represented by no-go, ablation, permutation, and blind-audit criteria.

## Still Open

- Full correlational wholeness across all sectors.
- Strict reconstruction of the underlying entity `S`.
- Complete classification of observables by reading regimes.

Verdict: the gap was mostly documentary, not numerical. Tome II now states the inheritance relation honestly and keeps the unproven Tome I ontology as II.B work rather than silently treating it as closed.
